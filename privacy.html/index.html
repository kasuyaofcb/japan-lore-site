Privacy Policy

Japan Lore Publisher · Last updated: 2026-07-15

Japan Lore Publisher is a private internal tool operated by Japan Lore Co. We use it only to upload our own original Shorts to TikTok @japanloreco and YouTube @japanloreco.

TikTok

We use TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API to authorize our own account and publish original videos. We store OAuth tokens (including refresh tokens) and the account open_id needed to post. Profile fields returned by user.info.basic are used only to confirm we authorized the correct account.

You may revoke access in TikTok’s connected-apps / authorization settings. After revocation, we delete stored TikTok tokens from our systems within 30 days (or sooner on request).

YouTube API Services

Our tool also uses YouTube API Services. By authorizing the tool for YouTube, you also agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.

Google’s general privacy practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

We collect and use YouTube account data only as needed to upload videos to our own channel (OAuth scopes such as https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload).

Data we store

  • OAuth tokens for TikTok and/or YouTube (channel / account owner only).
  • Video metadata we submit (title, description, privacy / visibility).
  • Internal pipeline logs needed to operate the publisher.

Data retention and deletion

You (the account owner) may revoke Google access in Third-party access and TikTok access in TikTok’s app permissions. After revocation, we delete stored OAuth refresh tokens within 30 days (or sooner on request).

To request deletion of authorization data, contact us via @japanloreco on TikTok or @japanloreco on YouTube. We will confirm deletion within 30 days.

What we do not do

  • We do not offer public accounts or a multi-user product.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not upload content on behalf of unrelated third parties.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact us via TikTok @japanloreco or YouTube @japanloreco.
