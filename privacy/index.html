Privacy Policy

Japan Lore Publisher · Last updated: 2026-07-14

Japan Lore Publisher is a private internal tool operated by Japan Lore Co. We use it only to upload our own original Shorts to our YouTube channel @japanloreco via the YouTube Data API.

YouTube API Services

Our tool uses YouTube API Services. By authorizing the tool, you also agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.

Google’s general privacy practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

We collect and use YouTube account data only as needed to upload videos to our own channel (OAuth scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload).

Data we store

  • OAuth tokens (client credentials + refresh token) for the channel owner.
  • Video metadata we submit (title, description, tags, privacy status).
  • Internal pipeline logs needed to operate the publisher.

Data retention and deletion

You (the channel owner) may revoke the tool’s access at any time in your Google Account’s Third-party access settings. After revocation, we delete stored OAuth refresh tokens from our systems within 30 days (or sooner on request).

To request deletion of data we store about the channel authorization, contact us via the @japanloreco channel. We will confirm deletion of tokens and related authorization records within 30 days.

What we do not do

  • We do not offer public accounts or a multi-user product.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not upload content on behalf of unrelated third parties.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact us via @japanloreco on YouTube.
